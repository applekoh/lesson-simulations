About this simulator

The system is the gas in the cylinder: 1 kg of air, treated as an ideal gas with constant specific heats: R = 0.287 kJ/kg·K, cv = 0.718 kJ/kg·K, cp = 1.005 kJ/kg·K and γ = cp/cv = 1.4. Each process is quasi-equilibrium and reversible, and kinetic and potential energy changes are neglected. PV = mRT is the same as PV = nrT, because mR = nr. Lower-case symbols are per kilogram (specific form, for example cv in kJ/kg·K); upper-case symbols are for the whole mass (for example Cv = mcv in kJ/K). Entropy S is measured from state 1 (S1 = 0).

Sign convention: ΔU = Q + W, with Q = Qin − Qout (Q > 0: net heat input; Q < 0: net heat output) and W = Win − Wout = −∫ P dV (W > 0: net work done on the gas; W < 0: net work done by the gas). Some textbooks use a different sign convention, so check which one a formula assumes before you use it.
