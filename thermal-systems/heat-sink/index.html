Why this simulator uses Case 2 (negligible heat loss from the fin tip)

Case 2 treats the fin tip as insulated: no heat leaves the tip, so the tip area is not counted and Afin = pL. This is reasonable here because the fins are thin: the tip is only a few per cent of each fin's surface, and very little heat is left by the time it reaches the tip. For the designs in this activity, Case 1 (convection from the fin tip) would give only about 3–4 % more heat per fin, so the answers barely change, while Case 2 gives a much simpler formula, ηfin = tanh mL / mL. Case 1 matters more for short, thick fins, where the tip is a bigger share of the surface. Case 3 (infinitely long fin) does not apply, because these fins are short (mL is well below 1).
